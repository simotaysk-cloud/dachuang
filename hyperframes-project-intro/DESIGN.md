## Style Prompt

Create a competition-ready project introduction for a Chinese medicinal herb traceability system. The visual identity should feel credible, technical, clean, and rooted in traditional medicine without becoming antique or decorative. Use precise data lines, soft botanical forms, and restrained interface-like panels.

## Colors

- Deep medicinal green `#0B2F24` for the primary canvas.
- Herb leaf green `#2FA36B` for process and product signals.
- Data cyan `#38BDF8` for digital traceability and verification.
- Warm verification gold `#D8A64B` for trust, quality, and certification.
- Rice white `#F5F1E8` for headline text.

## Typography

- Use system Chinese sans-serif fonts: `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, `Microsoft YaHei`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
- Headlines are compact and confident; supporting text is short and presentation-facing.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use neon cyberpunk colors or purple-blue gradients.
- Do not use ornate calligraphy or heavy traditional texture.
- Do not make the palette one-note green; balance green with cyan, gold, and rice white.
- Do not place text in crowded bubbles or oversized marketing cards.
